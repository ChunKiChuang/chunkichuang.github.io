I study how students conceptualise AI and how those conceptions shape the way they interact with AI systems.

I'm an MPhil researcher at the Faculty of Education, University of Hong Kong. I've published at AIED and have papers under review at CHI and AAAI.

I also build the tools I research with. At KindKernel I engineered a voice-based assessment platform (Futurlogue) that placed students in roleplay scenarios and used speech recognition and LLMs to evaluate skills like creativity and problem-solving from their spoken responses. At HKU I built a platform that helps teachers in bilingual programmes generate assessment items using LLMs.

Before the MPhil I studied linguistics at HKU. Outside research, I write fantasy fiction, make pour overs, and walk around cities with no particular destination.
