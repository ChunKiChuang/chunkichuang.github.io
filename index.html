I study how learners and teachers conceptualize and rely on AI systems, alongside counterfactual and psychometric audits of AI evaluators and explainability tools.

I'm an MPhil researcher at the Faculty of Education, University of Hong Kong. On the human side, I study self-regulated learning, feedback-sourced writing revision, and students' conceptions of AI across career fields; on the system side, I audit LLM grading contamination and post-hoc explanation faithfulness. I've published at AIED (oral), with solo- and first-author manuscripts in revision at IJAIED and under review at premier AI and fairness venues.

I also build the tools I research with. At KindKernel I engineered a voice-based assessment platform (Futurlogue) that placed students in roleplay scenarios and used speech recognition and LLMs to evaluate skills like creativity and problem-solving from their spoken responses. At HKU I built a platform that helps teachers in bilingual programs generate multimodal assessment items using LLMs.

Before the MPhil I studied linguistics at HKU. Outside research, I write fantasy fiction, make pour-overs, and walk around cities with no particular destination.
