System · 01

Multilingual Real-Time Assessment Platform

Research Engineer KindKernel Ltd. Jun 2025 – Mar 2026 Proprietary

The platform placed primary students in voice-based roleplay scenarios and used speech recognition and LLMs to assess soft skills — creativity, problem-solving, communication — from their spoken responses across three languages. Teachers received quantitative reports on competencies that traditional assessment cannot easily measure. The core engineering constraint was latency: real-time ASR and TTS on shared GPU infrastructure, with an LLM scoring pipeline that had to evaluate open-ended speech without contamination from adjacent inputs.

What I built

  • Engineered backend systems and optimised GPU-based inference pipelines for real-time speech processing across Cantonese, Mandarin, and English.
  • Built the LLM-based scoring module for open-ended spoken responses, with assessment rubrics grounded in educational research.
  • Designed the grading pipeline to isolate individual responses from peer context — the contamination problem I found here became my AAAI-27 paper.
