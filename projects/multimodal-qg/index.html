CLIL Assessment Toolkit

MPhil Thesis HKU Faculty of Education 2025 – present

Teachers in bilingual programmes (CLIL) assess students across English and Chinese using text, images, and diagrams, but writing good assessment items across languages and modalities is slow and inconsistent. This platform generates multilingual, multimodal assessment items using LLMs, grounded in a CLIL assessment framework I developed from analysing 956 public examination questions across Biology and Geography (HKDSE 2018–2023). Teachers review, edit, and align generated items to their curriculum.

What I built

  • Designed and built the full-stack application end-to-end in Next.js, deployed on Railway.
  • Integrated LLM APIs for curriculum-aware item generation across text and image modalities.
  • Built the assessment framework from a systematic analysis of 956 HKDSE questions, coding for cognitive demands, linguistic demands, and multimodal elements.
  • Currently piloting with 7 in-service teachers in Hong Kong secondary schools.
